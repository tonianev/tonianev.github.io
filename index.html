01 About

I build the engineering layer that makes ML usable — and the orgs that keep it running.

I founded Sanofi’s first AI engineering organization and grew it from zero to 70+ people across Toronto, Paris, and Boston — 7 direct reports, 30+ FTEs, 40+ contractors — reporting to the VP of R&D. Before that: Customer AI at Petco, and four years at Loblaw building the pricing, promotion, and segmentation platforms behind Canada’s largest retailer.

The throughline is simple: make good models easier to build, ship, and operate — then build the team and standards so it stays that way.

  • Reliability first. Design for repeatability from day one.
  • Platform leverage. Reusable systems over one-off heroics.
  • Explicit tradeoffs. Cost, speed, complexity, ownership — named and priced.
  • Compounding standards. Teams and defaults that improve over time.
