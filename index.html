01 About

Make good models easier to build, ship, and operate, then build the team and standards that keep it that way.

I work at Okta as a Principal Software Engineer, AI. At Sanofi I built the AI engineering organization from zero: 7 direct reports, 30+ FTEs, and 40+ contractors, reporting to the VP of R&D. Earlier: Customer AI at Petco (via ShyftLabs), four years at Loblaw across data engineering and MLOps, and audience-data pipelines at Numeris.
